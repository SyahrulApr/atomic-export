# EU Access2Markets — Product Requirements (Panduan Umum)

**Portal:** Access2Markets (European Commission, DG TRADE)
**Sumber utama:** https://trade.ec.europa.eu/access-to-markets/en/content/product-requirements
**Diakses:** 2026-09-02

**Catatan sifat sumber:** Halaman ini bersifat panduan umum/konseptual (statis, berhasil di-fetch penuh sebagai teks). Daftar persyaratan produk yang berlaku untuk kode HS/kategori produk tertentu (misal persyaratan teknis spesifik untuk kopi, tekstil, dsb.) tetap butuh My Trade Assistant — lihat bagian Gap.

---

## Pertanyaan Pembuka Halaman

> "What requirements must be met to ensure that products meet safety, health and environmental standards?"

## Definisi

> "Product requirements refer to essential conditions that products must meet in order to enter a market."

## Cakupan Aturan Teknis

Aturan teknis (technical rules) mencakup karakteristik produk seperti: **design, labelling, marking, packaging, functionality, atau performance.**

## Tujuan Regulasi

> "There are specific rules in place to ensure that products are designed to protect human health, safety or the environment."

> "In most cases, regulations define the desired objectives to be achieved or the hazards to be addressed, but it does not prescribe technical solutions." — regulasi umumnya berbasis tujuan (goal-based), bukan mendikte solusi teknis tertentu.

## Tiga Area Penerapan Persyaratan Produk

1. **Produk itu sendiri** — mis. mudah terbakar (flammability), sifat kelistrikan, higienitas.
2. **Proses produksi/manufaktur.**
3. **Kinerja produk** — mis. efisiensi energi.

## Pertimbangan Perdagangan Internasional

> "Product requirements vary widely from country to country and from trading bloc to trading bloc. It can be costly for traders to comply with different requirements in different markets."

**Harmonisasi lewat perjanjian dagang:**
> "EU trade agreements often harmonise technical rules and regulations and establish collaboration between standard-setting, testing, certification and accreditation bodies on both sides."

## CE Marking

> "In the EU market, the CE marking indicates that a product meets all the safety, health, and environmental protection requirements to be sold throughout the European Economic Area (EEA)."

## Arahan Tindak Lanjut

> "So, you need to check the product requirements for each region you export to." — pengguna diarahkan ke tool **My Trade Assistant** untuk detail spesifik per produk.

## Gap / Keterbatasan

- Halaman ini hanya menjelaskan **konsep umum** product requirements. Daftar persyaratan teknis konkret (SPS/TBT spesifik, batas residu, standar pelabelan tepat) untuk **produk tertentu berdasarkan HS Code dan negara tujuan spesifik** hanya keluar lewat **My Trade Assistant** (form interaktif — input negara ekspor, negara tujuan, kode HS) yang me-render hasil secara client-side (Angular SPA); tidak ada endpoint teks statis untuk semua kombinasi produk.
- Sub-halaman terkait (Chemical products, Pharmaceutical and cosmetic products, dll.) belum di-fetch detailnya pada sesi riset ini — hanya tercatat sebagai daftar link untuk penelusuran lanjutan bila diperlukan.
